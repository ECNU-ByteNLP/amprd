\section*{Ethics Statement}

\textsc{PRDWeaver} can influence real product decisions; incorrect or hallucinated requirements may cause wasted effort or user harm.
We recommend human review prior to adoption and emphasize that generated PRDs are drafts.
Our benchmark briefs do not contain personal sensitive data to the best of our knowledge, and we do not aim to infer personal attributes.
We release analysis code to support transparency and reproducibility.
