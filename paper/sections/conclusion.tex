\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented \textsc{PRDWeaver}, a modular multi-agent pipeline for PRD generation from structured briefs, and evaluated it under controlled ablations and strong baselines.
On a 15-brief benchmark, the full system significantly improves deliverability-oriented dimensions over a strong prompting baseline, while targeted ablations validate causal roles (e.g., table generation).
Future work includes human evaluation with product practitioners and stability studies across models and decoding settings.
